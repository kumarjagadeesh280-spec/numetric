Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and process

Is my data secure with an offshore provider? ▼
Yes, absolutely. We implement enterprise-grade security protocols, encrypted data transfers, secure file handling, and strict confidentiality agreements. Your clients' financial information is protected with the same rigor as any local provider. We understand the sensitivity of accounting data and treat it with utmost care.
How do you ensure compliance with Australian standards? ▼
We maintain deep expertise in Australian Accounting Standards, ATO requirements, BAS lodgement rules, and superannuation regulations. Our processes are built around these standards, and we stay current with regulatory changes. Every report and entry is reviewed for compliance.
What's your typical turnaround time for accounting services? ▼
Turnaround times depend on the service, but we aim for quick delivery without compromising quality. Monthly accounting: typically 5-7 business days. Payroll: within 2-3 business days. Financial statements: 7-10 business days. We can discuss specific timelines based on your needs.
Can you handle multiple clients or all my back-office work? ▼
Yes! We can support a single client or manage your entire back-office portfolio. We scale with your growth and can handle multiple clients simultaneously with dedicated account management for each relationship.
How do we integrate with our existing systems? ▼
We work with the systems you use, particularly Xero where we're L1 & L2 certified. We can also work with other accounting software and will establish secure, efficient data transfer methods and regular communication protocols.
What's the cost structure? ▼
We offer flexible pricing based on service complexity and volume. We'll work with you to understand your needs and provide transparent quotes. Most clients see significant cost savings compared to local outsourcing while receiving premium quality service.
How do you handle client onboarding? ▼
We provide a structured onboarding process: initial discovery, system setup, detailed documentation review, knowledge transfer, and a ramp-up period with close oversight. We ensure you're completely comfortable before full handoff.
