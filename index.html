Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our services and process

Is my clients' data secure with an offshore provider? ▼
Yes, absolutely. We implement enterprise-grade security protocols, encrypted data transfers, secure file handling, and strict confidentiality agreements. Your clients' financial information is protected with the same rigor as any onshore provider. We understand the critical importance of data security in the accounting industry.
How do you ensure compliance with different country requirements? ▼
We maintain expertise in the accounting standards, tax requirements, and regulatory rules of the countries we serve. Our processes are built around these standards, and we stay current with regulatory changes. Every report and entry is reviewed for compliance specific to your jurisdiction.
What's your typical turnaround time? ▼
Turnaround times depend on the service. Monthly accounting: typically 5-7 business days. Payroll: within 2-3 business days. Financial statements: 7-10 business days. We can discuss specific timelines based on your needs and your clients' requirements.
Can you handle multiple clients and full back-office work? ▼
Yes! We can support a single client or manage your entire back-office portfolio. We scale with your firm's growth and can handle multiple clients simultaneously with dedicated account management for each relationship.
How do I integrate NUMETRIC with my existing systems? ▼
We work with the systems you use, supporting all major accounting software platforms. We establish secure, efficient data transfer methods and maintain regular communication protocols to ensure smooth integration with your existing workflows.
What's the cost structure and pricing model? ▼
We offer flexible pricing based on service complexity and volume. We'll work with you to understand your specific needs and provide transparent quotes. Most firms see cost savings of 40-60% compared to local staffing while receiving premium quality service.
How does the onboarding process work? ▼
We provide a structured onboarding: initial discovery meeting, system setup and configuration, detailed documentation review, knowledge transfer sessions, and a ramp-up period with close oversight. We ensure you're completely comfortable before full handoff.
