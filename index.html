Why NumetricERP?

Because you should take control of your business — not the other way around.

A True ERP, Not a Patchwork

Every module is designed to share the same unified database layer. Production events automatically update inventory balances and post directly to sub-ledgers without manual data syncs.

Built for Indian SME Manufacturers

Unlike generic international ERP setups, NumetricERP is pre-configured to align with Indian tax structures, HSN classification, and FCO regulatory standards.

WhatsApp-Native Operations

Allows operators to update shift metrics directly through plant WhatsApp groups. The software automatically parses these updates to minimize data entry lag.

Affordable Per-Plant Pricing

Our licensing model is designed for SMEs, offering predictable per-facility flat rates rather than complex user-based billing structures.

Weeks, Not Months, to Onboard

Features pre-built seed, pesticide, and NPK fertilizer recipe templates to accelerate database configuration and deployment.

Accounting Heritage

Numetric's services history ensures the product database tracks continuous inventory values and tax metrics accurately, rather than just basic warehouse counts.

About Numetric

The Shift to Autonomous Agri-Input Operations

Numetric started as an offshore accounting firm serving global practices. Along the way, we saw how every Indian fertilizer, pesticide, and feed manufacturer was running their factories on Excel, paper ledgers, and WhatsApp groups — losing control of their own operations.

NumetricERP is our answer. A true vertical ERP, built specifically for how Indian agri-input factories actually operate. We're building it module by module — starting with the operations your factory floor needs today, expanding into HR, CRM, quality, and maintenance through 2026 and 2027.

Our mission is simple: help every manufacturer take control of their business.
